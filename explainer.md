# Explainer
Looking at DC's juvenile arrests data set, which includes date, charge and location of juvenile arrests, I found that overall, arrests have decreased by about 50% from 2011 to 2024. The biggest change happened from 2019 to 2020, and the arrest totals have not returned to pre-pandemic levels. There were increases in juvenile arrests between 2021 and 2022, and 2022 and 2023, then the total decreased again.

This data doesn't include juvenile population data, I would also bring in Census data to get an arrest rate to compare to raw counts.
