Juvenile arrests in Washington D.C. have decreased by 50% between 2011 and 2024. The steepest decrease was between 2019 and 2020, about a 44% drop. While arrest totals have fluctuated since 2020, they have not returned to pre-pandemic levels, though there was a 30% surge from 2022 to 2023.

This data doesn't include juvenile population data, I would bring in Census data to get an arrest rate to compare to raw counts.

I looked first for any monthly trends in juvenile arrest because I was curious if arrests correspond to the school year, but did not see any meaningful connections between seasons and arrest totals, so I moved on to see how total arrests have changed year over year. I was surprised to see such a steep decrease, especially in 2020, and thought it would be an interesting direction for further reporting.
